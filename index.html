about the journal
A raft is enough
to begin

Raft & Draft was founded on the belief that a first draft deserves a reader. We publish prose, poetry, and screenwriting by writers under 18 — in English and in Russian.

We're not looking for perfect. We're looking for honest. For the kind of writing that makes you feel something you didn't expect.
